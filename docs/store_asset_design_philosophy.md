# Quiet Arithmetic

Quiet Arithmetic is a visual system for calm numeric attention. It treats numbers as objects in a measured field: deliberate spacing, stable geometry, and restrained contrast make the puzzle feel approachable without becoming childish. The work should look meticulously crafted, with every curve, mark, and margin placed through careful judgment rather than decoration.

The palette is built from mist, deep green, warm gold, and clay. These colors communicate focus and progress while staying close to the in-app interface. The final assets must feel like the product of deep expertise: no stock imagery, no fake devices, no noisy effects, no promotional claims, and no fragile details that disappear at small sizes.

Form carries the identity. A connected numeric path is the primary visual language: dots, line segments, and board geometry suggest solving without explaining. Text appears only where it earns its place, as a short visual anchor rather than a paragraph. The best version feels painstakingly refined and quiet enough to remain premium in Google Play contexts.

The feature graphic should extend the app icon rather than duplicate it. It uses the real game surface as evidence of the experience, surrounded by enough breathing room that Play overlays and crops do not damage the focal point. The result should appear labored over by someone at the top of their field, with crisp edges, aligned geometry, and no temporary placeholder energy.
